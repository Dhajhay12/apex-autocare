From Two Bays To A Full Workshop

Apex AutoCare was founded by two mechanics who were tired of watching customers overpay for guesswork. The promise was simple: diagnose correctly, quote honestly, repair once.

Eighteen years later that promise still runs the shop. We publish our labor rates, we show you the failed part, and if a repair is not needed yet, we tell you - even when it costs us the ticket.
